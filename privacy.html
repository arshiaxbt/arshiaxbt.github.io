yt-studio privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-18.

yt-studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed: the signed-in channel's videos, upload status and analytics reports. Stored: one OAuth refresh token and analytics summaries on the owner's private server. Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

Revoke access at any time: Google account permissions. Deletion: stored data is deleted within 30 days of revoking access or on request via this site's issue tracker.
